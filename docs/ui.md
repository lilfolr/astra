# UI/UX Design Summary

## 1. Visual Identity: "Neon Industrial"
The interface is designed to feel like a high-tech tactical HUD (Heads-Up Display) found on a starship bridge, while remaining accessible through friendly terminology.

- **Color Palette:**
    - **Deep Obsidian:** Backgrounds to minimize eye strain.
    - **Cyan:** Primary actions and active systems.
    - **Neon Orange:** Warnings, active chores, and critical status.
    - **Acid Green:** Completed tasks and verified systems.
- **Typography:** Bold Sans-Serif for primary navigation; Monospace for technical readouts and the scrolling ticker.
- **Visual Elements:** Subtle scan lines, glowing borders, and neon accents that give the UI a "living" machine feel.

## 2. The Command Deck (Dashboard)
The primary screen serves as the tactical center for the family.

- **Family Status Bar:** A prominent, glowing progress bar at the top of the screen that indicates the overall health of the ship (home).
- **Chore Summary:** Real-time counters showing "Total Chores" vs "My Chores."
- **Scrolling Ticker:** A "Ship AI" news feed providing status updates and motivational messages.
- **Home Layout:** A list of active rooms (Modules) allowing quick access to room-specific details and chore management.
- **Stats Overview:** Direct view of the user's current Level (LVL) and Points (CR).

## 3. Chores (Mission Control)
- **Task Lifecycle:** Chores are presented as mission-like cards with difficulty badges (Easy, Medium, Hard).
- **Interactions:**
    - **Start:** Assigns a chore to the user.
    - **Done:** Moves the chore to "Under Review."
    - **Verify:** (Captain Only) Confirms completion and distributes rewards.
- **Checklists:** Detailed tasks within a chore provide micro-feedback as they are checked off.

## 4. Family & Crew
- **Roster:** A list of all crew members with their live stats (XP, Credits, Level).
- **Recruitment:** Captains can generate 10-minute expiring registration codes/QR codes to add new family members.
- **Access Control:** Captains can disable/enable crew members to manage access to the "Ship."

## 5. The Shop (Planned)
- **Reward Grid:** A visual inventory of parent-approved rewards.
- **Currency:** Uses "Credits" (CR) earned from completing chores.

## 6. Atmosphere & "The Nag"
- **The Ship AI:** Instead of parental nagging, the app uses system alerts.
- **Tone:** Immersive and professional. *"Captain, the Life Support systems are failing due to a Laundry overflow."*
